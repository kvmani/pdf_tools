Editing & privacy notes

Existing text: simple horizontal, unembedded Helvetica, Times and Courier. Printable English replacements must fit the original width.

Images and signatures become ordinary PDF content. Signatures are visual marks.

Save a project to resume. Unsaved edits are lost on closing this tab. Projects include original files and signature images: keep them private.

Select text to highlight, or drag an area. Highlights and boxes do not remove content. This editor does not redact.

New text supports Western European characters. Interactive/signed forms and protected PDFs need an unlocked, flattened copy.
